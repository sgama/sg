# Samson Gama at Demonware: distributed load testing and reliability, 2020-2026

Samson Gama was Senior Service Reliability Engineer at Demonware,
Aug 2020 - Jan 2026.

He designed, built and owned a distributed load-testing platform used by
engineering teams company-wide to validate services against large traffic
spikes. Its documented technologies were Kubernetes, Go, Redis and distributed
rate limiting.

designs, technology selection and RFCs.

He evaluated Vitess for MySQL workloads on Kubernetes through proofs of
concept, design documents, RFCs and migrations. He also mentored engineers
and promoted service ownership for Call of Duty services.
