# How has Samson demonstrated leadership and collaboration?

At Demonware, Samson Gama mentored junior engineers on implementation and
engineering practices, contributed to cross-team architecture reviews, and
reviewed Python service changes. He also wrote the RFCs and technical proposals
that moved MySQL workloads above 1 TB onto Vitess.

At Mastercard he led bi-weekly technical sessions on offensive security,
covering practical attack techniques and defensive engineering practices.

His resume also lists Strata Council President, leading a council representing
approximately 1,200 residents, coordinating stakeholders, meetings,
decision-making and communication of operational priorities.

As a Veazey Foundation Trustee, he serves on the board of a charitable
foundation providing full scholarships to incoming UBC students, contributing
to governance, financial decisions and long-term planning.

These establish mentoring, technical influence and governance experience, not
an asserted number of direct reports or a formal engineering management
position.
