## 🎯 How I Work

**🔁 Automate the second time**
If a workflow gets repeated, it gets encoded, observable, and recoverable.

**📊 Measure before optimizing**
Latency, throughput, cost, and failure rate. A change that feels cleaner but moves none of those numbers is a guess.

**🛠️ Design for imperfect inputs**
Good systems survive traffic spikes, bad data, and human mistakes. Production is the only environment that counts.

**🧪 Learn by shipping**
I pick up new tools by building something real with them, then writing down what broke. That is how this site's AI stack came together.

**🛡️ Security by design**
Threat models belong in the design review, not in a separate audit a quarter later.
