## Executive Summary: System-Wide Risk and Project Scope

The Strata's **seven elevators**—essential vertical transportation for all towers—are **over 20 years old** and utilize drive systems with components that are reaching **end-of-life status**. The critical failure of the Waterworks Tower #2 elevator was merely the catalyst that validated the high-risk assessment for the entire system.

This project is a mandatory, phased modernization of **all seven elevators** in the complex. Its objective is to eliminate the severe operational risk posed by obsolete parts, which would otherwise trigger an unpredictable and extended service outage across the property.

---

## Technical Deep Dive: The Obsolescence Crisis ⚙️

The system's core problem is the drive and controller components are now **obsolete** and **no longer available** from the manufacturer. This technical reality places the Strata in a state of mandatory, imminent modernization.

### The Triggering Event (The Waterworks #2 Failure)

The failure in the Waterworks Tower #2 elevator, which serves a **31-storey building**, demonstrated the technical fragility of the entire fleet.

- **Risk Validation:** Independent consultants (Otis and Solucore) confirmed the failure was a predictable outcome of system aging.
- **Outage Risk:** During any major failure, the technical lead time to restore service is projected to be **4-12 weeks** or even **exceed 12 weeks** due to parts scarcity.

### The Technical Upgrade Options for All Seven Lifts

The Strata Council was forced to evaluate two technically distinct, costly paths forward for all seven elevators:

| Option | Component Focus | Technical Scope | Long-Term Technical Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option 1: Core Drive Component Replacement** | Drives (e.g., replacement for **OVF30**) | Replace all seven drives and other key components. | **High Risk:** These components are **incompatible** with a future full modernization and would be rendered obsolete again, requiring the cost to be duplicated. |
| **Option 2: Full Modernization (Recommended Path)** | Drives and Controllers | Complete replacement of all seven systems with modern drives (e.g., **Quattro Drive**) and controllers. | **Low Risk:** Ensures regulatory compliance and technical stability for the next 20+ years. |

**Technical Incompatibility:** Consultants stressed that a piecemeal replacement (Option 1) would lead to **technical component duplication**, forcing the Strata to buy new incompatible drives that would later be scrapped for the final full modernization.

## Regulatory and Installation Timelines 📅

The scope of this project is a multi-year effort that is strictly governed by provincial safety regulations.

### Technical Safety BC (TSBC) Requirements

A full modernization of the seven-elevator fleet is complicated by mandatory provincial safety regulations for major alterations.

- **Major Alteration Permit:** Any significant system upgrade, such as implementing a **Quattro Drive** system, requires a **Major Alteration Permit** from **Technical Safety BC (TSBC)** for each individual elevator.
- **Mandatory Inspection:** The permitting process requires detailed engineering plans and mandatory on-site inspections by a TSBC Safety Officer before any elevator can be returned to service.

### Project Timelines and Outage Management

The project is structured to manage the risk of having multiple elevators out of service simultaneously.

| Phase | Duration/Notes | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical Outage Risk** | **4 to 12+ Weeks** (per lift failure) | The technical risk the modernization project aims to eliminate entirely. |
| **Component Lead Time** | **4 Months** | The estimated scheduling and delivery delay for any major repair or upgrade work after vendor approval. |
| **Installation Period** | **Phased, Multi-Year** | The full modernization of all **seven elevators** is a multi-year project, phased to minimize disruption to the complex. |
| **Contract Management** | **Ongoing** | The seven elevators are maintained under an ongoing **Otis contract** of approximately $11,000/month, with independent consultation provided by **Solucore**. |

## Decisions

The strata, LMS 2995 Marina Pointe, chose to award the elevator modernization contract to **Otis**.

The key timelines and project details for the modernization are as follows:

### **Project Timeline**

- **Contract Award:** September 2024.
- **Equipment Delivery (Waterworks):** Scheduled for May 22, 2025.
- **Site Work Commencement:** Expected to start around May 26, 2025.
- **First Elevator Outage:** One elevator at Waterworks was scheduled to be out of service for **32 weeks** starting around May 26, 2025.
- **Waterworks \#1 Inspection:** The inspection for the first Waterworks elevator was tentatively scheduled for October 20, 2025.

### **Modernization Plan**

- **Contractor:** Otis.
- **Sequencing:** The project involves the modernization of all **7 elevators** in the complex (three in Landmark, two in Water Works, and two in Yaletown Limited).
- **Crew Assignment:** Two crews were planned to work simultaneously on the **Waterworks** and **Landmark** projects.
- **Crew Transfer:** Once the first crew finished Waterworks, they were scheduled to transfer to **Yaletown**.
- **Work Hours:** Work on site was expected to be from 6:00 AM to 2:30 PM, with noisy work to be kept to after 7:00 AM.
